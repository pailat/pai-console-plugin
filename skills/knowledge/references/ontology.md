### `uses` (usa)

An action uses the data it creates, changes, or reads (properties.mode: create, update, or read); a screen uses the action, integration, or flow supplying a block of its content; a flow uses the screens and actions its steps pass through; an API design uses the entities of its schemas (x-pai-entity) and the components implementing its operations (x-pai-component), relationships it derives when saved.
